Applies to all plans

  • First charge date: UPI and Indian cards from T+1 (if the subscription is created before 9:00 PM, otherwise T+2), at most 14 days ahead. This demo schedules it at T+3, 3:30 PM IST.
  • Pre-debit notice: sent at least 24 hours before each debit. After it is sent, the debit amount can't change.
  • Authorization: ₹1 for UPI and card in this demo; always ₹0 for eNACH.
  • Changing the amount: create a new plan, then call the Manage Subscription API with CHANGE_PLAN. The new amount can't exceed the original plan's maximum amount.
  • Cards: Indian Visa, Mastercard and RuPay; international Visa and Mastercard.
  • Retries: UPI gets 3 automatic hourly retries; card and eNACH are not retried.
